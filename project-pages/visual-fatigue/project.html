Visual Fatigue & Functional Connectivity

Overview

This study examined how video motion and brightness relate to visual fatigue and task-based functional connectivity. Twenty-nine participants watched four video conditions during fMRI and rated their fatigue. Higher motion was associated with greater reported fatigue; brightness did not show a significant effect. Connectivity patterns predicted subjective fatigue, particularly in high-motion conditions.
